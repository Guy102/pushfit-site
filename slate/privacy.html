Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

The short version. Your trades never leave your device. Slate has no account, no login, and no server that stores your journal. We cannot read your data, because we never receive it.

Who we are

Slate is developed by Guy Ohayon ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Slate iOS app does with information, and what it deliberately does not do.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Slate is written to a database stored locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Trades, amounts, symbols, prices, quantities and fees
  • Notes, tags, setups and mood entries
  • Accounts, including prop-firm rule settings
  • Your rules and their evaluation history
  • Attached images

None of this is transmitted to us. There is no user account to create, so there is nothing to link this data to.

iCloud sync

If you enable sync, your journal is copied to your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. That storage belongs to your Apple Account. We have no access to it, and Apple's privacy terms govern it. Sync is optional and can be turned off at any time, after which your data remains on the device.

Usage events never include amounts, symbols, notes, account names or any financial figure. They are sent without a user identifier and without your IP address being stored.

What we never do

  • We never sell data. To anyone. Ever.
  • We never share data with advertisers. Slate contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • We never track you across other apps or websites. Slate does not use the advertising identifier and does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to track.
  • We never read your journal.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive a receipt confirming subscription status. We never receive your payment details, card number or billing address.

Slate never requests location, contacts, microphone, calendar or health data.

Your data is yours

You can export everything you have entered, in CSV or JSON, at any time, from Settings. Export is free at every tier, including after a subscription ends. You can also delete all data permanently from Settings, which removes the local database and any iCloud copy.

Children

Slate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and material changes are announced in the app's release notes.
