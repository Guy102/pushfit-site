A trading journal that knows the rules you trade under.

Log a trade in about ten seconds. See what actually works.

And if you trade a funded account, know exactly how much room you have left before you cross a limit that ends it.

Headroom, live

How much you can still lose today, and how close you are to your maximum drawdown. Warnings before you cross a limit, not after.

Ten seconds to log

One required field: the amount. Everything else is optional, forever. No field has ever blocked a save.

Your plan, checked

Write your strategy in your own words. Slate reads it back as rules you confirm — and nothing becomes a rule on its own.

A review that shows its working

Every line carries the number behind it and the sample size it rests on. Under five trades it says so, instead of inventing a conclusion.

Four promises you can check

  • You will not lose a trade. Saved locally, immediately. Automatic backups, and an integrity check every time the app opens.
  • Ten seconds to log. There are no required fields beyond the amount, and there never will be.
  • Export is always free. On the free tier. After a subscription ends. It is your data.
  • No ads, at any tier. And the price is shown before you pay, with a reminder before the trial converts.

There is no account and no server. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to be breached. Your trades are stored on your device, and the App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected because that is literally true.

What Slate does not do

No investment advice. No signals. No “buy” or “sell”. Slate cannot place a trade and is not connected to any brokerage that could.

No leaderboard, no public P&L, no social feed.

No AI that guesses. Every figure is arithmetic, and every claim shows the number and the sample size it came from.
